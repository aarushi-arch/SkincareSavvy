# Virtual Try-On - User Guide

## What is Virtual Try-On?

The Virtual Try-On feature helps you visualize exactly where to apply skincare products on your face. Using advanced face detection technology, it highlights the specific areas where each product should be applied.

## How to Use

### Step 1: Complete Face Analysis
1. Go to the Face Analysis page
2. Upload a clear photo of your face
3. Wait for the analysis to complete
4. Your face image is now saved for try-on

### Step 2: Browse Products
1. View your recommended products
2. Click on any product to see its details

### Step 3: Try On the Product
1. On the product detail page, click the **"👁️ Try On"** button
2. A modal will open showing your face
3. Green highlighted areas show where to apply the product
4. Each zone is labeled (e.g., "Left Eye", "Full Face")

### Step 4: Close and Continue
1. Click the **×** button or click outside the modal to close
2. Continue browsing other products

## Product Categories and Application Zones

### Eye Products
- **Eye Cream, Eye Care**
- Zones: Around both eyes
- Application: Gently pat around the orbital bone

### Full Face Products
- **Moisturizer, Sunscreen, Cleanser, Toner, Mask**
- Zones: Entire face
- Application: Apply evenly across all highlighted areas

### Targeted Treatments
- **Serum**
- Zones: Cheeks
- Application: Focus on cheek areas for maximum absorption

- **Spot Treatment**
- Zones: Nose
- Application: Apply to specific problem areas

- **Acne Treatment**
- Zones: Nose, Forehead, Chin (T-zone)
- Application: Focus on acne-prone areas

### Lip Products
- **Lip Balm, Lip Care**
- Zones: Lips
- Application: Apply directly to lips

## Tips for Best Results

### For Face Analysis
- ✅ Use good lighting
- ✅ Face the camera directly
- ✅ Remove glasses if possible
- ✅ Ensure your entire face is visible
- ❌ Avoid blurry or dark photos
- ❌ Don't use photos with heavy makeup

### For Try-On
- The green overlay shows the recommended application area
- Lighter green = gentle application
- Multiple zones = apply to all highlighted areas
- Labels help identify each zone

## Troubleshooting

### "No face image found" Error
**Problem**: You haven't completed face analysis yet
**Solution**: Go to Face Analysis page and upload your photo first

### "No face detected" Error
**Problem**: The system couldn't detect your face in the image
**Solution**: 
- Upload a clearer photo
- Ensure your face is well-lit
- Face the camera directly
- Remove obstructions (hair, hands, etc.)

### Try-On Button Not Working
**Problem**: JavaScript or network issue
**Solution**:
- Refresh the page
- Check your internet connection
- Try a different browser
- Clear browser cache

### Zones Look Wrong
**Problem**: Face detection may have issues with the image
**Solution**:
- Upload a new face photo
- Ensure the photo is high quality
- Try a different angle

## Privacy & Data

- Your face image is stored temporarily in your session
- Images are not permanently saved to our database
- Images are automatically deleted when you log out
- No face data is shared with third parties

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need to upload my face for every product?**
A: No! Upload once during face analysis, then try on any product.

**Q: Can I use someone else's photo?**
A: Yes, but it's designed for your own face for personalized recommendations.

**Q: How accurate is the zone detection?**
A: Very accurate! We use MediaPipe, Google's advanced face landmark detection.

**Q: Can I save my try-on images?**
A: Not yet, but this feature is coming soon!

**Q: Does this work on mobile?**
A: Yes! The feature is fully responsive and works on all devices.

**Q: How long is my image stored?**
A: Until you log out or your session expires (typically 2 weeks).
